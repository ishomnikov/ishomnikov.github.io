Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Who this covers

iVault Harness is a personal tool with a single user: its author. It is not offered to anyone else and collects no data from anyone else.

What it accesses

With the author's explicit consent, the application accesses Google Drive files and folders shared with the account it is authorised against, and the contents of a dedicated Gmail mailbox created for it. It requests no access to any other Google service.

Where data goes

All processing happens locally on one computer owned by the author. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored on that machine's local filesystem with restricted permissions. No data is transmitted to any server operated by the author, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, analytics or model training.

Retention

Files retrieved from Google Drive are written to local storage and retained at the author's discretion. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which immediately and permanently ends the application's access.

Contact

Enquiries: the address listed as the developer contact on this application's Google OAuth consent screen.
